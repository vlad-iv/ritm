CSV format

How to move readings into BP Rhythm from another diary, app or Excel spreadsheet.

In brief

  • You need a CSV file: one row per reading, column names in the first row.
  • Date, systolic and diastolic are required. Time, pulse and note are optional.
  • In the app: Settings → “Export and import log” → “Import from CSV”. If the file is not from BP Rhythm, the app shows the “Match the columns” screen: check which column means what and see how the first rows will go into the log.
  • Import is free. Loading the same file again does not create duplicates.

What the app understands by itself

  • Column names in English and Russian: “Date”, “Time”, “Date/Time”, “Systolic”, “SYS”, “Diastolic”, “DIA”, “Pulse”, “HR”, “Heart rate”, “Note”, “Comment”, “Irregular heartbeat”. No names — columns are guessed from the values.
  • Blood pressure in one column: 132/85 (or 132/85/68 with pulse).
  • Dates: 2026-09-01, 01.09.2026, 09/01/2026 and 01/09/2026, Sep 1, 2026. If the file doesn’t show which is the day and which is the month, the app asks.
  • Time: 07:30, 07:30:15, 7:30 PM — in its own column or together with the date. Without a time the reading is placed at noon.
  • Separator — semicolon, comma or tab; encoding — UTF-8 or Windows-1251. Put text that contains the separator in quotes: "after waking up, sitting".

What will not go into the log

Rows without a date or blood pressure and with impossible values: systolic not between 60 and 300, diastolic not between 30 and 200, systolic not above diastolic, pulse not between 30 and 250, a date in the future. Before importing, the app shows how many rows are skipped and why, with row numbers. Readings already in the log (same blood pressure within a minute) are not duplicated.

How to prepare your file

  1. Export the history from your previous app — usually “Export” or “Share” → CSV or Excel.
  2. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Keep one header row, then one row per reading. Extra columns (weight, mood) can stay — just don’t choose them.
  3. Save: Excel — “File → Save As → CSV UTF-8”; Google Sheets — “File → Download → CSV”.
  4. Send the file to your phone and load it: Settings → “Export and import log” → “Import from CSV”.

Imported readings are marked “manual”: you can delete them from the log like readings entered by hand.

BP Rhythm log format

“Export to CSV” saves the whole log; the file opens in Excel and loads back without questions. UTF-8 with BOM, semicolon separator, oldest first. Column names are in Russian:

ColumnContents
Датаdate, DD.MM.YYYY, phone time zone — for Excel
Времяtime, HH:MM — for Excel
SYSsystolic, mmHg
DIAdiastolic, mmHg
Пульсpulse, bpm; 0 — no pulse
Аритмияarrhythmia: да (yes) or нет (no)
Уровень ВОЗlevel shown by the monitor (empty for manual readings)
Тонометрmonitor or source app name
MeasureTimemoment of the reading in unix seconds — the app reads the time from it
DataIDrecord id — prevents duplicates
Заметкаnote
Меткиtags, comma-separated

You don’t have to convert your file to this format: the columns from “The simplest file” are enough.

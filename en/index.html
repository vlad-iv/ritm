BP Rhythm

Blood pressure and pulse diary for Android.

  • Any blood pressure monitor — scan its display with the camera or enter readings manually.
  • Charts, averages, morning and evening, WHO blood pressure categories, a PDF report for your doctor.
  • Writing to Health Connect.
  • Morning and evening reminders. The log is stored only on your phone.

Support

The easiest way is in the app: Settings → “Contact the developer”. Technical information that helps find the cause is attached to the email. Or directly: vladivv@bk.ru.

FAQ

Reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications and exact alarms: Settings → Reminders → “Allow exact time”. If the reminder still doesn’t arrive after the app is swiped away, remove battery restrictions there; on HONOR, Huawei and Xiaomi also enable auto-launch in the Android app settings.

The scanner can’t read my monitor

Tap “Not reading? Save frames for debugging” in the scanner and send them from the scanner to the developer — this helps teach the scanner your model. Meanwhile, enter the reading manually.
